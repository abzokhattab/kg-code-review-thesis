Conclusions

RQ1: Does KG improve reviews?

Yes. KG mode shows +68% improvement over Baseline across 10 PRs. 8 out of 10 PRs show KG improvement.

RQ2: Which criteria benefit most?

F4 (Breaking Changes) and test-related criteria show largest gains. Dependency information helps identify integration risks.

Best Approach

Hybrid mode achieves +97% improvement and wins on 7/10 PRs, combining structural and semantic context.
